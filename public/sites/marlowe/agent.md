## Policies
From https://hellomarlowe.com/pages/policies-faq:
- Walk-ins welcome; advance booking recommended. A credit card is required to hold all appointments and is charged at completion or on cancellation/no-show.
- Cancel free up to 24 hours before start; cancelling, rescheduling or downgrading within 24 hours is charged the full appointment value (groups 5 to 7: 48 hours; 8+: per contract).
- Appointments are held only 5 minutes past start; later arrivals may be shortened or cancelled and charged in full.
- Cashless (credit/debit, no checks); tips in cash or Venmo, 20% to 25% suggested. Leaving without paying = full amount + 20% gratuity charged to the card on file.
- Soak-off gel only (no hard/soft gel, dip, powder or acrylic removal). Children must be accompanied by an adult. No pets except service dogs. Salon gift cards are for in-store services only.
